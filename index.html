Privacy Policy for HomeEase and HomeEase Worker

Last updated: 11 June 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to both HomeEase and HomeEase Worker. HomeEase allows customers to request home services, while HomeEase Worker allows service providers/workers to receive service requests, manage jobs, and share availability/location during active service work.

By using our applications, you agree to the collection and use of information described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Phone number
  • Email address, if provided
  • Profile photo
  • Address or service location
  • Current location
  • Worker profile and service category
  • Worker verification information, if required
  • Service request and booking details
  • Payment status and billing details
  • Device information and push notification token
  • App usage data and crash/performance logs

2. Customer App Data

In the HomeEase Customer App, we may collect customer information such as name, phone number, service location, selected service category, booking details, and active request status. This information is used to create service requests and connect customers with suitable workers.

3. Worker App Data

In the HomeEase Worker App, we may collect worker information such as name, phone number, profile photo, service category, availability status, location, completed jobs, and verification information if required. This information is used to show worker availability, send service requests, manage active jobs, and improve service reliability.

4. Location Data

HomeEase uses location data to provide location-based services.

For customers, location is used to find nearby workers, create service requests at the correct address, and track the assigned worker during an active job.

For workers, location is used to show online availability, receive nearby service requests, and help customers track worker arrival during active jobs.

5. Background Location for Workers

HomeEase Worker may collect worker location when the worker is online or handling an active job. This is used to show worker availability, receive nearby service requests, and help customers track worker arrival.

Location is not used when the worker is offline. Workers can disable location permission from their device settings, but some app features may not work properly.

6. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Connect customers with nearby workers
  • Show available workers on the map
  • Send and manage service requests
  • Track active service jobs
  • Send important app notifications
  • Verify worker identity and service eligibility
  • Manage invoices, payments, and service records
  • Prevent fraud, misuse, and unauthorized activity
  • Improve app safety, reliability, and performance

7. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information between customers and workers only when required to complete a service request. For example, a customer’s service location may be shown to the assigned worker, and a worker’s name, profile photo, category, and location may be shown to the customer during an active job.

We may also use trusted third-party services such as authentication providers, database and hosting services, maps/location services, push notification services, analytics tools, crash reporting tools, and payment providers if used in the app.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention

We keep user information only as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal requirements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and maintain business records.

10. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account and personal data by contacting us at the email address below. After receiving a deletion request, we will delete or anonymize personal data unless we are required to keep certain information for legal, security, fraud prevention, or business record purposes.

11. Children’s Privacy

HomeEase and HomeEase Worker are not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. User Rights

Users may contact us to access, correct, or request deletion of their personal information. Users may also disable permissions such as location access from their device settings.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
